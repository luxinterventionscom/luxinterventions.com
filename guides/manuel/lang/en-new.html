8 · 🏨 Hotel, Airbnb and “My stay”

Hotel and Airbnb

Rooms, guests and their app

The Hotel and Airbnb sections work the same way: the hotel has rooms, Airbnb has flats let by the night. Each guest gets their app, My stay, with a personal code.

1. Hotel settings

Hotel → ⚙️ Settings (button at the top):

FieldWhat it is for
Name of the hotel / property, AddressThey appear in the guest’s app and are used to calculate the taxi route.
Reception phone, WhatsAppThe 📞 Call and 💬 WhatsApp buttons in the guest’s app.
Taxi: pick-up fee, price per km, fixed prices airport / stationThe estimate in km and € that the guest sees before confirming the taxi.
Our drivers: name, phone (WhatsApp), car, plateTo send your own driver instead of a taxi firm.

2. Rooms and flats

  1. Hotel → ＋ Room (Airbnb: ＋ Flat).
  2. Room no. (Airbnb: No. / name of the flat, with the address), Floor.
  3. Category and Type (single, double, suite, studio…), Guests (max.), Area (m²), Number of rooms.
  4. Description for your guests: the text they see in their app.
  5. Check-in inventory: up to 7 photos, one per box, with 📷 Photo.

In the list each room is free or occupied.

3. A guest

  1. Hotel → ＋ Guest (also in Airbnb).
  2. First name, last name, phone, email, country (with the flag), language, guests.
  3. Arrival → Departure with the times, the room (or flat), the badge / key and the door code (visible to the guest only on the day of arrival).
  4. Board: room only, breakfast, half board, full board; preferences / allergies; 🍽️ the restaurant menu (PDF, the same for all guests).
  5. Save: the code for the My stay app is created; send it by 💬 WhatsApp, ✉️ Email or 📱 SMS.

The page shows 🛬 Arrivals today, 🛫 Departures today, 🛏️ In house, 📅 Upcoming and 🗂️ Departed. Room already taken on the same dates: the app warns you.

Check-out inventory

Before leaving, the guest sends the check-out photos from their app (one per box): they appear next to the check-in photos, to compare. They protect the deposit and the property.

4. The “My stay” app

The guest opens the link received and types their code. They see:

BoxWhat it contains
Your stayArrival and departure with times, guests, board.
Your room / Your flatFloor, rooms, m², description, photos, badge and door code (from the day of arrival).
🍽️ See the menuThe menu as a PDF.
How are you arriving?🚗 I’m coming on my own or 🚕 Send me a taxi (see below).
🚘 Book a driverDuring the stay: a ride, by the hour, half day, full day or the whole stay; car 💎 Luxury, 🚘 Comfort or 🚕 Economy; extras (bodyguard, drinks, child seat, name sign, luggage help) and the estimated total.
📋 Check-out inventoryThe photos before leaving.
📞 · 💬 · 🧭Call reception, WhatsApp, directions.

5. Arriving by taxi

  1. The guest chooses 🚕 Send me a taxi, the place (✈️ airport, 🚉 station or another address), When? and the flight / train no.
  2. The app shows the estimate: km, minutes and price in €. Confirm.
  3. You see it in the guest’s card: send the ride to a taxi firm (part 9) or choose one of your drivers.
  4. The driver gets a link (nothing to install): “Leaving to pick up the guest”, “I’m here”, “Guest on board”, “Arrived”. The guest sees the arrival time; the property sees the time of arrival at the hotel.

Privacy

Only the arrival time is shared, never the exact position. Sharing stops on arrival.

11 · 📣 HORECA ads — the “My ads” app

Ads from restaurants and bars

Restaurants, bars, nightclubs (HORECA) publish their own ads, menus of the day and events in your apps. You approve them before they go out.

1. Creating an advertiser

  1. Subscriptions → 📣 Advertising → ＋ Advertiser: name, contact details.
  2. An access code is suggested: save and send it with the app link.

2. The “My ads” app

  1. ＋ Ad, menu or event: Type (📣 Ad, 🎉 Event, 🍽️ Menu of the day), Title, Message / offer, address for the map, phone, website, video or music.
  2. Position (🔝 Top, ⏺ Middle, 🔽 Bottom) and In which apps? (🏠 Tenants, 👷 Teams, 🏢 Managers, 🏨 Hotel / Airbnb guests, 🚕 Taxi).
  3. From / To, the days (none = every day) and the hours (empty = all day).
  4. The price is calculated automatically → Send for approval.

Status: ⏳ Waiting for approval · ✅ Online · ❌ Declined (with the reason) · ⌛ Ended. Under each ad: views and clicks.

Payment

After your approval the advertiser pays by bank transfer (QR code) and taps ✅ I have paid; the ad goes online when you confirm the payment (see part 13). The 💳 Pay online (card) button is visible but not connected yet.

3. Approving

A new or changed ad arrives at the office as “📣 to approve”. Open the advertiser: ✅ Publish or ❌ Decline with the reason, which the advertiser reads in their app. If the ad has a price, after approval it goes into “💶 To collect” and goes online on the chosen dates, days and hours as soon as payment is received (part 13).

12 · 🔑 Licences and client offices

Licences

A separate, encrypted office for each client

You can give your clients — managers, agencies, owners, hotels, Airbnb — their own office: separate from yours, encrypted with their key (you don’t know it), with only the sections of their sector.

1. Rates

Settings → 🔑 Licences → 💶 Rates: for each sector the price for one year and the discount for 2 and 3 years (My properties stays out).

2. A new client

  1. ＋ Client: sector, name, phone, email.
  2. The client starts with a fixed 15-day trial and gets an invitation code (valid 30 days, once only).
  3. They open their office with the link, choose their key and start.

3. Subscription, invoices, expiry

Button / statusWhat happens
1 year · 2 years · 3 yearsThree buttons with the price of each package (discount included). The PDF invoice is created, marked paid or to pay.
🟢 ActiveThe client works normally.
🟠 Read-only (expired)On expiry: they see their data but cannot change it.
🔴 BlockedThe office no longer opens.
New accessNew invitation code (key lost).
DeleteDeletes the client’s office and issues a deletion certificate (PDF with fingerprint).

The Licences page also shows the statistics (clients, on trial, active, takings), the log and Export CSV.

4. The client’s office

The client sees their name at the top, the sections of their sector and a trial or expiry banner. They manage their own properties; the jobs are still done by the main office: in Requests → ＋ Request they write subject, urgency, address, description and contact. The request arrives encrypted directly in your Maintenance.

5. The client’s data belongs to the client (GDPR, terms)

The data in a client’s central (guests, tenants, payments, invoices, messages) is encrypted with the client’s key. Neither you nor anyone else can read it: the server only holds unreadable bytes. You only receive what the client chooses to send: intervention requests and payment of your invoices.

StageWhat happens to the data
🟠 Read-onlyInvoice unpaid beyond the chosen delay (Read-only after, 30 days by default) or subscription expired: the client sees everything and can make an Encrypted backup, but cannot change anything. Pays → unlocked automatically.
🔴 BlockedCan no longer log in, but the data stays intact and encrypted. Reactivated → everything is back.
🗑️ Delete with certificateDirector only, by typing the client’s name. Everything is deleted for good; the certificate remains (date, objects, fingerprint).

📜 To write in the terms and conditions: the client is responsible for their backups · if unpaid: read-only → notice (e.g. 30 days) to download → deletion with certificate · on request we hand over an encrypted copy (only they can open it) · lost key = data unrecoverable by anyone · if the business is sold, the buyer takes over the obligations towards clients. Have the text checked by a local lawyer or accountant.

13 · 💬 Team chat and 💶 Payments

Team chat

Each worker talks only with the office

In the team app there is the 💬 Messages with the office card. The worker writes, the office replies: the other workers do not read these messages. Messages are encrypted like everything else.

  1. The worker writes in their app and taps Send. Until the message has gone they see “⏳ sending…”.
  2. The office sees on Home “💬 new message” with the worker’s name. Tap it: their card opens on the 💬 Chat tab.
  3. Reply in the box at the bottom: the worker gets the notification and reads the reply in their app.

Special messages to a group

  1. Settings → 💬 Team group (special messages): tick the workers who belong to the group.
  2. Maintenance → team → 📣 Special message to the team: the group is already ticked; you can remove or add someone just for this message.
  3. Write and send: each recipient sees it in their chat with the label 📣 Special message. Those not in the group don’t see it.

House messages: 🔒 private, ✓✓ blue, edit

  1. 🔒 Private — only for the office: the tenant ticks the box before sending. Only they and the office read it; the others don’t see it. From the central: 🔒 Reply privately or “To: 🔒 Private — name”.
  2. Ticks on your own messages: ✓ sent · ✓✓ reached the office · ✓✓ blue read by the office. Also in the team chat.
  3. ⋯ on your own message, for 15 minutes: ✏️ Edit · 🔒 Make private / 🔓 Make public · 🗑️ Delete. Others see “edited”; the office sees the previous text (edited · see before).

⚠️ Archive: a red WARNING box explains what disappears from the lists, and that invoice numbering restarts at 0001 only if you tick “🧾 Invoices and quotes” (now unticked by default).

How payment works

For now by bank transfer: the invoice shows IBAN, BIC, reference and a QR code to scan with the banking app. Whoever has paid taps ✅ I have paid; you check the account and confirm. The card button is visible but not connected yet.

1. Your billing details

Settings → 🧾 Invoicing of interventions: company name, address, company no., VAT no., IBAN, BIC, bank, VAT rate, payment term. At the bottom:

FieldWhat it is for
Late notice after (days)Days after the due date when the client sees the “invoice not paid” notice (default 7).
Read-only after (days)Days after the due date when the client’s office goes read-only (default 30). It unlocks by itself as soon as everything is paid.

2. Job invoices to your clients

  1. The client chooses once in Settings → 💳 Payments: For each job or One invoice per month (summary).
  2. You finish the job and enter hours, rate, travel, materials (Accounting → 🧾 Invoices). A 0 € invoice is refused with a clear message.
  3. Licences → the client → 🧾 Invoice N finished job(s): the PDF invoice is created (number YYYYMMDD-INT0001) and arrives encrypted in the client’s office.
  1. The client finds it in Requests: they open the invoice, see the amount, the transfer QR and download the PDF.
  2. They pay and tap ✅ I have paid. You see on Home “the client says they have paid”: check the account and tap ✅ Received.
  3. If they don’t pay: after 7 days the notice, after 30 days read-only (they see their data but cannot change it; they can always read the invoices and tap “I have paid”).

3. Taxi: invoices in the taxi firm’s name

On first opening the taxi app, the firm fills in 📋 Our company and invoices: company details (name, address, company no., VAT), how it wants to be paid (IBAN transfer, payment link or to the driver), the price policy and billing:

ChoiceWhat it means
Price from the rates (estimate)The estimated price of the request applies.
I confirm the price when acceptingThe firm writes the price when it accepts the ride.
I correct the price after the rideThe firm writes the final price at the end of the ride (waiting, tolls…).
One invoice per ride / One monthly summaryOne invoice per ride, or one invoice a month with all the rides.
  1. Each driver has a driver code 🪪, visible on the ride at the office.
  2. At the end of the ride the firm taps 🏁 Ride finished; the invoice (number TX-YYYY-0001) is created in the taxi firm’s name, or at the end of the month.
  3. At the office, Taxi: for each firm the statistics (rides, accepted, declined, finished, ⭐ average) and the Taxi invoices (to be paid directly to the company). Tap Pay: the firm’s IBAN and QR, or its link. Then Paid.
  4. The firm sees the invoice “paid” in 🧾 Our invoices and taps ✅ Received. Each invoice prints with 🖨️ Print.

Guest rating: once the ride is finished, the guest gives 1 to 5 ⭐ and a comment in their app. The rating is seen by the hotel / Airbnb and by the taxi firm.

4. HORECA ads: approve, collect, publish

  1. The 💶 Rates for the spaces (🔝 top, ⏺ middle, 🔽 bottom) are per day, excl. VAT; the VAT is the one in 🧾 Invoicing.
  2. Approve the ad: in the advertiser’s app it becomes 💶 Approved — to pay with the amount incl. VAT, the transfer QR and ✅ I have paid.
  3. At the office the ad is in 💶 To collect, with “paid according to the advertiser” once they have tapped the button.
  4. Check the account and tap ✅ Payment received: the invoice is created (number YYYYMMDD-ANN0001) and the ad goes online. The advertiser prints the invoice from their app (🧾 Invoice).
