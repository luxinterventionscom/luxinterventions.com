Property management · Real estate · Hotel · Airbnb · Taxi

Who uses it and why

One app, many doors: at the centre, jobs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for property managers, room landlords, hotels, Airbnb and estate agencies — linked to our team, partner firms and occasional workers.

🏢 Management and officeManagement app — receives requests, assigns the work, follows the sites, makes quotes, invoices (QR, “I have paid”), accounts and statistics.
👷 The teamTeam app — in-house workers, partner firms, occasional workers: planning, “I’m on my way”, before / after photos, hours, “How many?”, 💬 chat with the office.
🏠 The tenantsTenants app — report a problem with photos, see cleaning and bins, documents, rents, ads and the door code.
💼 Managers and co-ownershipsManagement portal — a request in 30 seconds, every step with notifications, quotes to accept, invoices to download.
🏨 Hotels and AirbnbHotel and Airbnb sections — rooms, guests, arrivals and departures; guests have the My stay app (door code, menu, taxi).
🚕 Taxis“Rides” app — partner firms accept rides, with drivers, cars (luxury, comfort, economy) and rates; invoices in their name, paid directly by the hotel.
🏘️ Agencies and ownersAgencies and Owners sections — portfolio, investment funds, properties to let and for sale with photos.
📣 Restaurants and bars“My ads” app — ads, menus and events in the apps: approval, payment (QR), online.
🔑 Licensed clientsClient’s own office — their encrypted office: 15-day trial, then 1, 2 or 3 years; job invoices with QR.

One application, one group, one city, one nation!
